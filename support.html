Getting Started

  1. Open IGTT and choose Schedule Exit.
  2. Choose or enter the caller identity.
  3. Select the caller voice and reason for calling.
  4. Choose Immediate Exit or Escalating Urgency.
  5. Select when the exit should begin.
  6. Tap Schedule Exit.

For scheduled exits to appear when IGTT is not open, notifications must be enabled for the app.

Notifications

IGTT uses notifications to deliver scheduled simulated incoming calls when the app is in the background, closed, or the device is locked. If a scheduled exit does not arrive, first confirm that notifications are enabled for IGTT in your iPhone or Android settings.

Scheduled Exit Not Arriving

  • Confirm notifications are enabled for IGTT.
  • Confirm the scheduled exit appears on the IGTT home screen.
  • Confirm the scheduled time has not already passed.
  • Cancel the exit and schedule a new one.
  • Restart the app and try again.
  • On Android, verify the app is allowed to deliver time-sensitive/full-screen call notifications if your device presents that setting.

Audio Problems

  • Check the device volume.
  • Confirm audio is not being routed to Bluetooth headphones, speakers, a vehicle, or another connected device.
  • Disconnect external audio devices and try again.
  • Confirm the device has an internet connection when using cloud-generated voice audio.

Voice Audio

IGTT uses text-to-speech technology for caller audio. If voice playback does not start, confirm that your device is connected to the internet, then retry the exit or voice preview.

Important Safety Information

IGTT is not an emergency service. It does not place a real telephone call or contact another person on your behalf.

IGTT is a personal safety and convenience tool that creates simulated call experiences. It should not be relied upon as a substitute for contacting emergency services, law enforcement, medical professionals, trusted contacts, or other real-world assistance.

If you are in immediate danger or experiencing an emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service for your location.
