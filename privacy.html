I Gotta Take This — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: To Be Added Before Public Launch

I Gotta Take This (“IGTT”) is a mobile application developed and operated by Golden Fusion Enterprise, LLC (“GFE,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the I Gotta Take This mobile application.

Information We Collect

The current version of I Gotta Take This is designed to operate primarily on the user’s device.

IGTT does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the app’s core features.

IGTT may store certain information locally on the user’s device as necessary to provide app functionality. This may include scheduled exit scenarios, caller and persona selections, voice selections, timing information, and app preferences.

Notifications

IGTT may request permission to send notifications.

Notifications are used to provide scheduled app functionality, including alerting users when a scheduled exit is ready.

Users can control notification permissions through their device settings.

Audio and Speech

IGTT uses audio functionality to provide simulated call experiences, sound effects, and related app features.

The current version of IGTT does not record or upload user audio.

Audio used by the app consists of bundled app sounds and device-generated speech used to provide the simulated call experience.

Information Sharing

IGTT does not sell users’ personal information.

The current version of IGTT does not provide user information to third parties for advertising purposes.

The current version does not use third-party advertising services or analytics services.

If future versions introduce analytics, cloud services, artificial intelligence services, account systems, advertising, or other third-party services, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain those practices.

Data Storage and Retention

Information used by IGTT is stored locally on the user’s device as necessary to provide app functionality.

The current version of IGTT does not operate a user account system or store user information on servers operated by Golden Fusion Enterprise, LLC.

Removing the app may remove locally stored app information, subject to the behavior of the device operating system and any applicable device backup services.

Device Information and Other Data

The current version of IGTT does not access the user’s contacts, location, camera, or photo library as part of its core functionality.

IGTT does not currently use third-party advertising or analytics technologies to track users across apps or websites.

Children’s Privacy

IGTT is not specifically directed toward children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We take reasonable measures to design and operate IGTT in a manner appropriate to the information handled by the app.

However, no electronic system or method of storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as IGTT evolves.

Changes may be made to reflect new app functionality, technologies, legal requirements, or business practices. The effective date of the Privacy Policy will be updated when changes are published.
